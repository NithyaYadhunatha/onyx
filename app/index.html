Set up once

  1. On the phone: Settings → About phone, tap Build number 7 times.
  2. Developer options → USB debugging on, plug into this PC, tap Allow. Done.
  3. Or without a cable: Wireless debugging on (same Wi-Fi), tap Pair device with pairing code, and fill in the form here.

Once connected, Onyx can use your phone's apps, and logs bank SMS into your expense sheet every 15 minutes.
